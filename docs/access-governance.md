# Access Governance

## Overview

Access governance ensures the right people have the right access to the right resources at the right time.

## Access Certification

Access certification is the process of regularly reviewing user access rights.

This helps confirm users still need their access.

## Segregation of Duties

Segregation of duties prevents one person from having excessive control over a critical process.

Example:

One user should not be able to both create a vendor and approve payments to that vendor.

## Access Modeling

Access modeling provides visibility into who has access to what resources.

This helps identify excessive permissions and risky access combinations.

## Risk Analytics

Risk analytics identifies high-risk access patterns, such as:

- Privileged access combined with sensitive business access
- Dormant accounts with active permissions
- Contractors with broad access
- Conflicting duties

## Key Takeaways

- Access governance reduces excessive access.
- Reviews help prevent privilege creep.
- Risk analytics helps prioritize remediation.
- Segregation of duties reduces fraud risk.

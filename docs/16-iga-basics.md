# Identity Governance and Administration Basics

## Overview

Identity Governance and Administration, or IGA, combines identity lifecycle management with access governance.

IGA helps ensure users have appropriate access while maintaining compliance and security.

## Key Components

### User Provisioning and Deprovisioning

Automating the creation and removal of user accounts.

### Access Certification

Regularly reviewing and validating user access rights.

### Role-Based Access Control

Assigning permissions based on job roles.

### Policy Enforcement

Ensuring access rules are applied consistently.

### Segregation of Duties

Preventing one person from having excessive or conflicting access.

### Audit and Compliance Reporting

Generating reports on user access and activity.

## Key Takeaways

- IGA connects lifecycle management and access governance.
- Access should be reviewed regularly.
- Automation can reduce delays and errors.
- IGA supports compliance and risk reduction.

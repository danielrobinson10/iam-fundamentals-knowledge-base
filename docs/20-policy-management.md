# Policy Management

## Overview

Policy management ensures access decisions are consistent, secure, and aligned with business requirements.

## Policy Types

### Access Policies

Control who can access specific resources.

### Password Policies

Define password complexity, expiration, and security requirements.

### Segregation of Duties Policies

Prevent conflicting responsibilities.

### Compliance Policies

Support regulatory and organizational requirements.

## Policy Creation

Policy creation may include:

- Risk assessment
- Rule definition
- Exception process
- Approval workflow

## Policy Enforcement

Policy enforcement includes:

- Preventive controls
- Detective controls
- Automated remediation
- Violation handling

## Key Takeaways

- Policies define how access should be granted and controlled.
- Exceptions should be documented and approved.
- Enforcement helps reduce inconsistent access decisions.
- Automated remediation can reduce response time.

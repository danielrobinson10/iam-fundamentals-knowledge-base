# PAM Best Practices

## Overview

PAM best practices help organizations reduce the risk of privileged account misuse or compromise.

## Discovery and Inventory

Maintain a complete inventory of all privileged accounts across systems.

This helps prevent hidden accounts from becoming unmanaged risks.

## Credential Rotation

Privileged credentials should be rotated regularly and automatically when possible.

## Separation of Duties

No single administrator should have unchecked control over critical systems.

## Continuous Monitoring

Organizations should monitor for unusual privileged account activity.

Examples:

- Login from unusual location
- Access outside normal hours
- Repeated failed attempts
- Use of emergency accounts
- Unexpected privilege escalation

## Least Privilege

Users should have only the minimum privileges needed to perform their job.

## Key Takeaways

- Inventory is the foundation of PAM.
- Password rotation reduces credential risk.
- Monitoring helps detect suspicious activity.
- Separation of duties reduces insider risk.

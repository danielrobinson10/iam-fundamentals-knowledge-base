# PAM Risk Register Template

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Domain Admin | Full environment compromise | High | Vaulting, MFA, session monitoring | Monthly |
| Service Account | Hidden standing privilege | High | Ownership, rotation, access review | Quarterly |
| Break-Glass Account | Emergency misuse | Critical | Monitoring, limited use, alerting | Monthly |
| Database Admin | Sensitive data access | High | Approval workflow, logging, least privilege | Monthly |
